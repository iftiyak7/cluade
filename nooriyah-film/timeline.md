- **No fabricated claims:** there is no NOORIYAH packaging, product label, price, offer, certification, testimonial, address, map, phone number or website. All jars carry blank kraft labels. The outlet interior is an illustration, not the real shop.
- **Audio:** the voice-over is unaltered. Under it sit an original synthesised score (pad, felt piano, soft plucks) and sparse sound design (air swells, room tone, a honey drop, a grinding rustle, pours, a glass tap). The bed is ducked about 5.5 dB under speech and sits about 15 dB below the voice overall.

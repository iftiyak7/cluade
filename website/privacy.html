Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Summary

Hayat is designed so that your personal data stays on your device. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service.

Data on your phone

Tasks, routines, reminders, health logs, prayer logs, notes, journal entries and settings are stored only in Hayat's private app storage on your phone. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for this data.

Announcements

If Announcements is turned on (the default), the app downloads a public list of tips and updates from our server about once an hour while you are online. The request contains only the app's version number in the User-Agent and a cache tag; it contains no identifiers, cookies or personal data. Our server sees your IP address as part of any internet connection; it is used only in memory to prevent abuse and is not logged or stored. Turn this off in Settings → General → Announcements, which also deletes downloaded announcements.

Backups

Backups are files you create and choose where to save. They can be encrypted with your password (AES-256-GCM). We never receive them.

Permissions

Hayat asks for notifications and exact alarms (for reminders), and optionally biometrics (for app lock). It does not request location, contacts, SMS, storage or advertising identifiers. Prayer times use a city you choose.

Children

Hayat does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

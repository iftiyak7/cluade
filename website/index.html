Questions

Does Hayat need the internet?

Only to connect and sync. Every feature works offline; changes are saved on your phone and synced to your server when you are online.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone, and — once you connect — on the Hayat server you chose. You can also export an encrypted backup file.

Is the Islamic module required?

No. It is optional and can be turned on or off in Settings.

Will reminders always ring on time?

Hayat uses Android's exact alarms when allowed and shows you if your phone limits them. Some battery savers can delay alarms; the app explains how to fix that.
